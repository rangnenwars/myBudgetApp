# Prapanji Google Ads: first campaign draft

Draft for review, not yet entered in Google Ads. Every headline is at most 30 characters and every description at most 90 (checked by script). The limits, the policy position and the cost figures are in the chat notes of 2026-11-05; confirm them in Google Ads before spending.

## 1. Set-up

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Publicity: get Prapanji known and visited. Revenue and cost per sign-up are not the measure |
| Campaign 1 (this file) | Search: reaches people already looking for an expense or budget app, so every click is a real visit |
| Campaign 2 (later) | Demand Gen or YouTube reach with images or a short video, to put the name in front of people who are not searching yet. Needs section 6 |
| Not yet | App campaigns: the app is web-only and not on Google Play |
| Landing page | https://prapanji.in. One page, loads fast on a slow phone, says in one line what Prapanji is |
| Locations | India. Start with Maharashtra if you want a smaller test |
| Languages | English first. Hindi ad group after the first two weeks, checked by a native speaker |
| Budget | About ₹300 to 500 a day for 3 to 4 weeks (₹6,000 to 15,000). Lower is fine: the aim is visits, not a volume target |
| Bidding | Maximise clicks with a max-CPC cap, so cheap visits are favoured. Add a brand campaign on "prapanji" at a very low cost |
| Networks | Search only for Campaign 1. Turn off Search Partners and Display expansion |
| Devices | All. Check the page on mobile first |
| Tracking | Optional but worth it: GA4 on prapanji.in so you can see visits and which ad group brought them |

## 6. Images and logos (needed for the awareness campaign)

Not needed for Search, but Campaign 2 (Demand Gen or YouTube reach) depends on them, and for publicity they matter most. Confirm the sizes in Google Ads when you upload. Google's Reach Planner gives estimated reach and cost for a budget before you commit.

- Square logo 1:1 (ideally 1200×1200) and wide logo 4:1 (ideally 1200×300), from your icon in `assets/icon.png`.
- Images: 1.91:1 (1200×628), 1:1 (1200×1200), 4:5 (960×1200).
- Screens to show: Home with "Money left after fixed bills", Budgets, Split a bill, People. Use demo data, round numbers, and very little text.
- Do not show real user names, emails or amounts.

## 7. What the ads and page must not say

- Only claim what the app does today: tracking, budgets, repeating bills, EMI tracker, splitting, lend and borrow notes, goals, reports.
- Say "Free to start", not "Free forever". Prices may be set for some features later.
- Do not offer or imply loans, credit, investments, tax advice, guaranteed savings or returns. The Loans and Investments screens only record the user's own details.
- Do not advertise Plan ahead or Pro features to everyone: they are switched on per user by an admin.
- Keep a clear privacy policy and terms link on the landing page, and a contact email.
- Before launch, read Google's financial services policy for India and decide whether verification applies to this product.

## 8. Measure publicity, not revenue

| Measure | Where | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions and share of searches shown | Google Ads | How many people saw the name |
| Clicks, click rate (CTR), cost per click | Google Ads | Whether the message interests people, and how cheap each visit is |
| Visits and how long they stay | GA4 | Whether the visit was real |
| Searches for "Prapanji" | Google Trends, Search Console | Whether the name is sticking. This is the best publicity signal |
| Sign-ups | Your admin Users screen | A side effect to watch, not the goal |

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| Day 1 to 3 | Check the ads are approved and the page works on mobile |
| Every 2 to 3 days | Read the search terms report, add negatives, pause keywords with a high cost per click and no interest |
| Week 2 | Move budget to the ad groups with the best click rate and lowest cost per click |
| Week 3 to 4 | Compare searches for "Prapanji" before and after. Decide whether to add the image or video campaign |

Cost per visit is the number to keep low, and it is easy to compare between ad groups. A rising count of people searching your name tells you the publicity is working.

If you want publicity without paying for clicks, the same assets can also go on a free Google Business Profile or a short video, but that is outside Google Ads.
