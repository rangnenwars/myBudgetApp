# Prapanji — User Manual

Prapanji helps you see where your money goes: record income and spending, track loans and EMIs, investments and savings goals, set monthly budgets, and see reports and your net worth — in rupees (₹), on your phone or in any web browser.

**Open the app:** https://prapanji.in (works in any modern browser on phone or computer — nothing to install). If your organisation also gave you an Android app, it works the same way; see [USER_DISTRIBUTION.md](USER_DISTRIBUTION.md).

## 1. Getting started

### Create your account
1. Open https://prapanji.in and tap **Create an account**.
2. Enter your **name**, **email** and a **password of at least 8 characters**.
3. Tap register. You are signed in straight away.
4. **Confirm your email.** We send a link to your address. Until you open it, a yellow banner on the Dashboard reminds you (everything still works). No email? Go to **Settings → Send confirmation email**, and check your spam folder.

There is no guest mode — an account is how your data is kept safe and available on every device.

### Sign in and out
* **Sign in** with the email and password you registered. Passwords are **case-sensitive**.
* **Sign out** with the log-out icon (top right of the Dashboard).
* **Forgot your password?** Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen, enter your email, open the link we send you and choose a new password. (If no email arrives, ask your administrator to reset it for you.)

### Moving around
Six tabs along the bottom (or side, on a wide screen):

| Tab | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | This month at a glance, shortcuts, alerts |
| **Transactions** | Every income and expense; add, edit, search |
| **Loans** | Loans and EMIs, part payments |
| **Investments** | What you've invested and how it's doing |
| **Goals** | Savings goals and progress |
| **Reports** | Trends and charts (Pro) |

Icons at the top of the Dashboard: **bug** = report a problem · **gear** = settings · **log-out** = sign out · (staff accounts also see a **shield**).

## 2. The dashboard

* **Month switcher** — the ‹ › arrows step back and forward through months.
* **Income / Expense / Net savings** for the month shown.
* **Budgets** and **Accounts** shortcuts.
* **Budget alerts** — categories at 80 % of their limit turn yellow, over 100 % turn red. Tap to open Budgets.
* **Top expense categories** — your five biggest spending areas.
* **Budget class badge** — a label (low, middle, high…) the app works out from your recent spending. It updates by itself.
* **Free-money day** — the date by which your income has paid for every EMI and repeating bill; every day after it is yours to spend. Tap it for the breakdown and a "what if I prepay a loan" preview (section 2a).
* **Orange plus button** — add an income or expense in a few taps (section 2b).
* **Input expenses** button — fast monthly entry (section 4).
* **Pro insights** — month-over-month changes and your savings rate (Pro).

### 2a. Free-money day

Your loan EMIs and repeating bills are spoken for every month. The app adds them up, compares them with your monthly income, and shows the date by which your income has covered them all. If 61% of your income is committed, that date is the 19th of a 31-day month, and the last 12 days are free to spend.

* **Income** is your repeating income entries; if you have none, it is the average of your last full months.
* The card compares with last week ("2 days earlier") once it has a reading from the week before.
* Tap the card for each commitment and its share. Under **What if you prepay a loan**, type an amount to see how the loan's EMI and your free-money day would change. This is a preview only; record the real part payment under Loans.
* "No free days" means your fixed bills and EMIs are as much as your income. "Not enough to work out yet" means no income is recorded.

### 2b. Quick add

Tap the orange **+** on the dashboard: pick Expense or Income, type the amount on the keypad, tap one of your most-used categories (or **More**), and **Save**. It is recorded for today. **Add note** adds a note; **Split across categories** opens the full form with your amount filled in.

## 3. Transactions

**Add one:** Transactions tab → **+** → choose **Income** or **Expense** → pick a **category** → enter the **amount** → (optional) a note → **Save**.

* **Category** — tap the field for the list (Household, Food & dining, Transport, Health, Family, Loans & EMIs, Credit cards, Shopping, Property, Investments & savings, Income…). Use the search box to find one fast. Can't find yours? Add your own **custom category** from the same list (you can rename or delete your own later; a category that still has transactions can't be deleted).
* **Split across categories** — one payment, several categories (a ₹2,000 shop that is ₹1,200 groceries and ₹800 household). Switch it on, give each line a category and an amount, and add lines as needed; **Left to assign** must reach ₹0 before you can save. Each line is saved as its own entry, so budgets and reports count every category correctly. A split is for a single payment, so it can't repeat or use a quarterly/yearly period.
* **Split with people** — you paid a bill for friends (₹1,800 dinner for you, Rahul and Priya). Switch **Split** on, choose **With people**, pick a category, tick the people (or type a new name), and choose **Equal** or **Custom** shares. The summary shows **Your share**, which is the only part counted as your spending, and what each person owes you. They then appear under **People** (section 9a). Any odd paise stay with you so the total always adds up. It's for expenses.
* **Date** — today by default. Choose *Yesterday* or any past date. Future dates aren't allowed.
* **How often** — *Once* records the exact amount on that date. *Monthly / Quarterly / Yearly* converts the amount to its monthly equivalent (a ₹12,000 yearly bill is recorded as ₹1,000/month).
* **Repeat** — switch it on for salary, rent, insurance or any fixed amount. The same entry is added automatically every month, quarter or year on the same day (29th–31st use the month's last day). To change the amount, the frequency or the day — or to stop it — tap the **Repeating** button.
* **Edit** — tap a row, change it, save. **Delete** — the trash icon (you'll be asked to confirm).
* **Find things** — search by note, category or amount; filter **All / Expenses / Income**. The list loads 50 at a time as you scroll.

## 4. Input expenses (fast bulk entry)

Best for entering a month's spending in one go, like filling a spreadsheet.

1. Dashboard → **Input expenses**.
2. Choose **Monthly**, **Quarterly** or **Yearly**.
3. Add one row per category with its amount (add or remove rows as needed).
4. **Save** — every row becomes a transaction (quarterly/yearly amounts are divided to a monthly figure).

## 5. Loans

Loans tab → **+**. Enter name, principal, outstanding balance and EMI; optionally interest rate, tenure, start date, loan type, lender and a note.

* **Add monthly EMI to expenses** (on by default) — each month the EMI is added to your expenses as *Loan EMI*, and the loan's balance is reduced by that month's principal (interest is worked out from the rate). Nothing to tap.
* For loans you don't want counted as spending (a family loan, say) switch it off and use **Mark EMI paid** yourself.
* **Part payment** — paid extra? Enter the amount; the balance drops and the EMI scales down so your remaining tenure stays the same. A before/after preview is shown first.
* Each card shows the next EMI's interest/principal split and months left at the current rate. Tap a card to edit; trash icon to delete. Outstanding loans count against your net worth.
* **Debt payoff planner** (Pro) — compare *snowball* and *avalanche* strategies and see "what if I add ₹X a month".

---

## 6. Investments

Investments tab → **+**: name, type, amount invested, current value, optional start/maturity dates and a note. The app calculates the **gain %** and shows a portfolio summary. Tap a card to edit; trash icon to delete. Keep **current value** up to date for an accurate net worth.

---

## 7. Goals

Goals tab → **+**: name, target amount, optional deadline, colour and icon.

* **Contribute** to add (or take out) money; each action is kept in the goal's history.
* The progress bar shows how close you are. Tap a card to edit; trash icon to delete.
* **Goal ETA** (Pro) projects a completion date from your recent savings rate.

---

## 8. Budgets

Dashboard → **Budgets**. Set a **monthly limit** for any expense category. A progress bar shows how much of this month's limit is used; you get an alert on the Dashboard at 80 % and again when you're over.

---

## 9. Accounts

Dashboard → **Accounts**. Record where your money sits — **bank**, **cash**, **wallet/UPI** — and what you owe on **credit cards**. You type the balances in yourself (they are not calculated from transactions); update them whenever you like. They feed your net worth (card dues are subtracted).

---

## 9a. People (money you lend or borrow)

Dashboard → **You get / You owe** card (or the **People** link). Use it for money lent to or borrowed from friends and family. Lending is **not** counted as spending, so your reports stay accurate.

* **Add person or IOU** — choose **I lent** or **I borrowed**, pick a person or type a new name, enter the amount and, if you like, a pay-back-by date and a note.
* **People list** — each person with what you get or owe. Filters: **All**, **You get**, **You owe**, **Overdue**. Tap a person for their page.
* **They paid / I paid** — records a payment. Part payments are fine; you can't record more than is owed.
* **Lend or borrow** — add another amount for the same person.
* **Due date** — set or change the pay-back-by date. A balance past its date shows **Overdue**.
* **Write off** — clears what's left when you've decided not to chase it. It stays in the history.
* The trash icon on a line removes a mistake and recalculates the balance. **Delete person** removes them and their whole history.

Your friends don't need Prapanji, and nothing is sent to them. Your bank balances aren't changed either: update them under Accounts if you want them to match.

## 10. Reports and Pro features

The **Reports** tab and a few extras are **Pro** features — **included for everyone**, nothing to switch on or pay for:

* Income / expense / net trend chart and category breakdown — pick this month, 3 months, 6 months, a year or all time
* **Export to CSV** (share or download the file)
* Debt payoff planner, goal ETA, spending insights
* **Net worth** — investments + goal savings + account balances − loans − credit-card dues, tracked month by month

## 11. Settings and your account

Dashboard → gear icon.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send confirmation email** | Re-sends the email-confirmation link |
| **Change password** | Enter your current and a new password (8+ characters). Other devices are signed out. |
| **Download my data** | Saves everything in your account — transactions, loans, investments, goals, accounts, budgets, and the problems you reported — as one file you keep |
| **Sign out everywhere** | Lost a phone or used a shared computer? Signs you out on every device at once |
| **Delete my account** | Permanently removes your account and **all** your data. Needs your password. Download your data first if you want a copy. This cannot be undone. |

## 12. Report a problem

Something broken or confusing? Dashboard → **bug icon**.

1. Choose a **category** (bug, crash, wrong data, display, slow, other) and **where** it happened.
2. Choose how serious it is (low → critical).
3. Give it a short **title** and describe what happened. Adding *steps to reproduce* and *what you expected* helps a lot.
4. Optionally attach a **screenshot** (PNG/JPEG/WebP up to 2 MB). Never include passwords in a screenshot.
5. **Send.** The app version and device type are attached automatically. Your earlier reports and their status (new → triaged → resolved) are listed below the form.

**Who sees your report.** The team reads the title and description to fix the problem. A **screenshot can show your balances**, so only you and the app's administrators can open it (support staff can't), and it's deleted 90 days after the problem is marked fixed or closed. Your reports are deleted with your account.

## 13. Privacy and security

* Your data is private to your account. Administrators can see account details (name, email, role, status) to run the service — **not** your transactions, loans, investments or goals. The one exception is a screenshot you choose to attach to a problem report (see §12).
* Your password is stored only as a one-way hash; nobody, including the administrators, can read it.
* The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Sessions expire quickly and refresh on their own; **Sign out everywhere** ends them all immediately.
* Use a password that is unique to this app. Never share it. Staff will never ask for your password.

## 14. FAQ and troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Invalid email or password" | Passwords are case-sensitive; check Caps Lock and the email spelling. Still stuck → **Forgot password?** |
| My account is deactivated | An administrator turned it off. Contact them. |
| I didn't get the confirmation or reset email | Check spam; request it again from Settings / the sign-in screen; if none ever arrive, tell your administrator (email may not be configured). |
| Numbers look wrong on Reports | Reports follow the month/range you picked. Check the range at the top, and that investments' *current value* and account balances are up to date. |
| A recurring entry is wrong | Transactions → **Repeating** → change or stop it. Entries already added stay; edit or delete them individually. |
| I deleted something by mistake | Deletions can't be undone. Re-add it. |
| Page shows an error or won't load | Refresh. If it persists use **Report a problem** (if you can open it) or contact your administrator. |
| Is my data backed up? | Yes — the service is backed up nightly, but keep your own copy with **Download my data** from time to time. |

## 15. For staff accounts (admin, support, system manager)

Staff accounts are ordinary accounts with an extra **shield** icon on the Dashboard. What opens depends on the role:

| Role | Shield opens | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | **Users** | See every account; search by name/email; switch accounts **Active/Inactive**; change **Role** (user, support, system manager, admin) and **Tier** (Standard/Pro); **delete** an account permanently; open the **Audit log** and **System metrics** |
| **Support** | **Users** (limited) | Switch regular users **Active/Inactive** only. No role/tier changes, no deleting, can't touch staff accounts. |
| **System manager** | **System metrics** | Read-only totals: users (active/inactive), signups and logins, usage counts, cost/revenue estimate. Never sees individual accounts. |

Good practice:
* You can't change or delete **your own** account from the Users screen (to prevent locking yourself out). Use your own login's Settings.
* **Deactivating** blocks sign-in and ends the person's session within minutes — use it before deleting.
* **Deleting** removes the person and all their data for good; the audit log keeps who did it and when.
* Every role/tier/active/delete action is recorded in the **Audit log** (latest 100 shown).
* Keep at least **two** active admins. To make the very first admin, or fix a locked-out one, see [DB_ADMIN_QUERIES.md](DB_ADMIN_QUERIES.md) §2.
* Unconfirmed emails are tagged in the Users list; grant Pro by opening the user and choosing **Pro**.
